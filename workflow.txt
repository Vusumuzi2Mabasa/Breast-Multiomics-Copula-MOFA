1. SCOPE
All data, gene IDs, genomic positions, LD and TNBC labels are synthetic.
The current v3 experiment includes germline genotype and RNA only. There are
no protein measurements, somatic mutation features or copula models in v3.
The previous v1 and v2 experiments remain preserved. Their READMEs and
workflows are archived under baseline/ and v3/baseline/, respectively.
This is a case-only TNBC versus non-TNBC illustration, not susceptibility GWAS,
real breast cancer eQTL discovery or a clinical biomarker validation study.

2. DESIGN
24 genotype dosages in six correlated four-variant regions and 12 mapped RNA
genes. Two training sizes, 240 and 480. Four scenarios: null, shared eQTL,
subtype eQTL with RNA abundance shifts, and abundance-only without eQTLs.
200 eQTL replicates per cell: 1,600 cohorts. Ten integration replicates per
cell: 80 independent training/test pairs, each with 360 separate test cases.
Dedicated pilot seeds are excluded from the evaluation banks. Source hashes
were frozen before evaluation; seeds and effect sizes are not selected for
favourable results. See v3/protocol/design.json and source_lock.json.

3. DATA GENERATION
v3/src/simulation.py generates synthetic ancestry, age, cell fraction, batch,
genotype and case subtype. Local latent Gaussian correlation is 0.65^distance;
inverse binomial thresholds give dosage 0/1/2. This is synthetic LD, not a
public African reference. RNA latent log2 abundance has cis genotype effects,
subtype effects, genotype-by-subtype effects, three shared residual programmes
and noise. Negative-binomial counts have dispersion 30 and a known simulated
whole-assay library. Analysis uses log2(CPM+1), not counts divided by the sum
of only twelve panel genes. All equations are in the README and Word report.

4. MOFA AND DIABLO
Official mofapy2 engine, two Gaussian working views: standardised genotype
and RNA. Ancestry and batch adjustment and scaling are fitted using training
participants only, with no subtype labels in MOFA representation learning.
K=2/4/6 is selected by 15% masked genotype/RNA reconstruction, equally weighted
by view. Masked values are excluded from preprocessing estimates. Two genuine
starts (PCA and jittered PCA) are selected by training ELBO. Refitted MOFA
scores enter a fixed C=1 ridge logistic classifier plus observed clinical inputs.

Official R mixOmics block.splsda fits genotype/RNA molecular blocks plus a
clinical-input block. Threefold stratified training validation selects one or
two components and keepX four or eight. Preparation is repeated within each
fold. The additional clinical inputs provide the same information supplied
to MOFA's ridge classifier; they are not a third molecular assay. Official
weighted-centroid voting gives class labels. DIABLO is compared by balanced
accuracy, not artificial ROC scores from hard classifications.

PCA, genotype-only ridge, RNA-only ridge, combined ridge and clinical-only
ridge are included. All workflows use identical independent test participants.
Convergence flags remain visible and are not silently excluded. Ten replicates
per cell provide limited precision; these are workflow comparisons, not proof
of general algorithm superiority or transport to a new clinical cohort.

5. CIS eQTL MAPPING
For each gene, jointly fit its four local genotypes and their interactions
with subtype, adjusting for age, cell fraction, synthetic ancestry and batch.
The omnibus test compares full versus covariate-plus-subtype models (eight
additional coefficients where full rank). The interaction test compares full
versus common-genotype models (four additional coefficients). Observed design
ranks determine F-test degrees of freedom. Linear tests on transformed counts
use a working Gaussian reference; repeated null scenarios assess realised error.

Apply BH separately across twelve genes for each family within each cohort.
No joint FDR guarantee across the two families is claimed. Evaluate sensitivity
against gene-level truth and mean false discovery proportions, including zero
when no genes are selected. LD tag associations are not labelled false causal
variants. SNP-level example associations are nominal illustrations, not a
fine-mapping analysis. No colocalisation, mediation, causal discovery or pQTL
analysis was performed. Subtype interactions require direct interaction tests;
significance in one subtype only is insufficient.

6. QUALITY CHECKS AND EXAMPLE
v3/data/example contains 240 prespecified subtype-eQTL cases, genotype/RNA/count
matrices, metadata, covariates, libraries, synthetic positions and truth tables.
QC checks dimensions, finite values, valid dosages, positive libraries,
nonnegative integer counts and minor allele frequency. HWE summaries are within
ancestry and use a Pearson approximation only when all expected counts >=5.
These checks do not replace real genotype, RNA-seq, relatedness or imputation QC.
There is no FASTQ processing or actual genomic coordinate annotation here.

10. VALIDATION
Six unit tests check deterministic replay, the two data layers, BH known values,
F-test scale invariance, planted interaction signs, train-only preparation and
official MOFA projection with distinct initialisations. Eight complete eQTL
cohorts and one full integration replicate are replayed. Independent R QR calculations check all twelve genes in
eight fixtures, their two test families and BH arithmetic. R independently
checks AUC, balanced accuracy and Brier for all methods in the first integration
replicate of every scenario/size cell. Python recomputes probability metrics
for all 480 ridge/projection method records and verifies every DIABLO voted-class
metric against the common independent test outcomes. Every selected factor
budget and every pair of initialisation hashes are checked. Prior releases are
verified byte-for-byte. Word pages are rendered and visually inspected before
release; QA images and internal PDFs remain in the ignored work directory.

The preserved abundance-only N480 ordinary-F omnibus control yielded 11%
false discoveries. A disclosed amendment added HC3 sensitivity and 800 fresh
null/abundance-only cohorts without replacing original results. The original
HC3 cell remained elevated at 8%; fresh ordinary-F and HC3 cells were 5.5%
and 4.5%. HC3 also rejected in 8.5% and 9% of fresh complete-null
cohorts at N240 and N480. Neither reference is certified for confirmatory use
without further calibration. These do not establish universal error control. Additional HC3
checks were independently validated in R. See robustness_summary.csv and
protocol/robustness_amendment.json.

12. LIMITATIONS AND REAL STUDY TRANSITION
Synthetic LD and idealised covariates do not establish validity in an African
clinical cohort. The small gene family and strong effects do not provide a
transcriptome-wide power calculation. The subtype-eQTL scenario also changes
RNA abundance, so prediction cannot isolate interaction effects. Real Study 3
needs assay confirmation, ancestry/relatedness adjustment, expression QC,
clinical covariates, adequate interaction power and independent replication.
Blood findings do not establish tumour origin. Real colocalisation would require
harmonised external GWAS/eQTL statistics and suitable LD; none is substituted
by these simulated results.
