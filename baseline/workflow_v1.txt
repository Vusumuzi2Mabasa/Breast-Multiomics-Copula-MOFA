COPULA AND MOFA INTEGRATION OF SIMULATED BLOOD MULTIOMICS
Author: Vusumuzi Mabasa

1. SCOPE
This is a synthetic-data methods benchmark. No real TACA, Clin-MAP or patient
data are used. It is a feasibility study for blood-based breast cancer Study 3.
The scripts do not call, classify or interpret clinical genomic variants.

2. PROJECT STRUCTURE
protocol/design.json: prospective numerical design.
protocol/source_lock.json: frozen design, numerical-source hashes and versions.
src/simulate.py: four-view synthetic generator and train-only standardisation.
src/models.py: official MOFA+ interface and custom Gaussian copula Gibbs sampler.
src/benchmark.py: fitting, independent test projection and phenotype prediction.
src/run_all.py: bounded parallel execution and dependence companion experiment.
src/dependence.py: independently calibrated, prespecified RNA/protein pair tests.
src/analyse.py: complete result tables, paired uncertainty and figures.
src/validate.py: deterministic fixed-fixture replays and R validation inputs.
src/check_metrics.R: independent AUC, average precision, Brier and subspace checks.
src/manuscript.py: research README generated from numerical tables.
src/build_word.py: Quarto manuscript and Word report with editable equations.
src/reproduce.py: full rerun into a new directory without deleting prior results.
tests/test_models.py: generator, ordering, invariance and correlation tests.
results/replicates: 360 fixed replicate JSON records, fixtures and MOFA logs.
results/dependence: calibration banks, thresholds and fresh evaluation records.
figures: publication PNG and SVG figures.
report: Quarto source and final Word report.
work: ignored dependencies, logs and internal rendering QA.

3. NUMERICAL DESIGN
Six mechanisms x 2 training sizes x 30 replicates = 360 independent training/test
pairs. Training sizes: 180 and 360. Independent test size: 200 per replicate.
Features: 6 germline dosages, 6 somatic indicators, 24 RNA and 12 proteins.
Methods: mixed MOFA+, Gaussian MOFA+, copula, rank PCA, covariates and oracle.
Mean-signal outcomes depend on two of three latent backbone factors.
Dependence-only outcomes instead alter one RNA/protein correlation.
Non-TNBC is the synthetic comparator; all subtype labels are generated.
The copula has two chains, rank-constrained mixed-data latent updates and proper
priors. A fixed diagnostic rule extends sampling when monitored split Rhat >=1.1.
The rank-PCA and copula projections learn empirical margins from training only.
MOFA uses Gaussian transformed RNA/protein and an explicit Bernoulli somatic view.
All test outcomes remain outside feature fitting, scaling and classifier fitting.
Classifier penalty C=1 and latent factor count 3 are fixed, not test-tuned.

8. INDEPENDENT DEPENDENCE TEST
python src/dependence.py
At each N, 500 null calibrations determine a strict threshold, then 500 fresh
null and 500 fresh alternative datasets evaluate it. Two statistics use identical
datasets. There is one prespecified pair and no discovery-based feature search.
Thresholds use ascending rank 476/500; averaged null bound is 25/501.
The test is separate from factor fitting and does not establish causality.
The initial N=180 rank-normal statistic had an 8% fresh null rejection rate.
Its results and calibration bank are preserved. A prospective uniform extension
in protocol/dependence_validation.json increases calibration to 5000 per N and
uses fresh 1000-dataset evaluation banks for rho=0, 0.25, 0.40 and 0.65.
The strict rank is 4751/5000 and averaged null bound is 250/5001. These fresh
results are primary; results/dependence retains the initial experiment.
Run: python src/dependence_validation.py

10. INTERPRETATION
Compare factor recovery, prediction and dependence testing separately.
Report paired differences with uncertainty, computational costs and diagnostics.
The primary recovery metric is dimension-aware: absent estimated dimensions
contribute zero and the denominator remains three. Pre-release independent R
checking detected an optimistic legacy metric in the raw replicate records.
The original records are preserved; src/analyse.py applies src/metrics.py and
records the full amendment in results/recovery_correction.csv. Use
results/evaluation.csv for primary recovery results. Simulation and prediction
outputs were not changed. See protocol/metric_correction.json.
The oracle is a simulated reference, not a practical method or guaranteed bound.
The raw 'Clinical only' method includes both clinical and technical covariates.
Rare alterations, missingness, fixed factor count, small panels and limited
replicates constrain conclusions. No clinical or causal claims are supported.
